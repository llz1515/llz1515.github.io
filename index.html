Research Interests

  • LLM Agents: Developing language-model-based agents for reasoning, planning, and multi-step task execution.
  • Embodied Agents: Combining visual observations, memory, and interaction feedback to act in simulated environments.
  • Multimodal LLMs: Integrating visual and language information for multimodal understanding and reasoning.
  • Agent Memory: Building memory systems that retain, update, and retrieve experience for long-horizon tasks.

Bio

I am a master’s student in Electronic Information at Zhejiang University. I received my bachelor’s degree in Software Engineering from South China University of Technology in 2026.

My research focuses on LLM Agents, Embodied Agents, and Multimodal LLMs, with an emphasis on agent memory, reasoning, long-horizon task execution, and multimodal understanding. I led EmbodiedMemory-Bench, from benchmark and environment development to memory methods, model training, and evaluation.

I previously interned at Tencent IEG, working on Unreal Engine 5, UGC tools, and graphics programming. I also enjoy competitive programming and received a Gold Medal at the 2025 ICPC Asia Shenyang Regional Contest.
